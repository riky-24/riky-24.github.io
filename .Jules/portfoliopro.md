## 2024-05-22 - Hero Section Overhaul
**Learning:** First impressions are made in the first 10 seconds. A generic "Software Engineering" title with broken links and mixed languages immediately signals "unfinished" to recruiters.
**Action:** Implemented a strong Hero Section with a clear Value Proposition ("Building responsive, scalable web applications...") and working CTAs. Removed deprecated Bootstrap classes to ensure consistent spacing. Standardized navigation to English to target a global audience.
